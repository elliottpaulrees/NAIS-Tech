## What is 3D Modelling?

3D modelling is the process of creating a digital representation of a three-dimensional object using specialized software. It allows designers to build and visualize objects from any angle, modify them easily, and prepare them for physical production. 
3D modelling is widely used in industries like animation and video game deveopment to create objects and creatures which can be added to the digital world. It is also often used in architecture, engineering and product development to create real world objects using a 3D printer.


## What is 3D Printing? 
3D printing, also known as additive manufacturing, is a process of creating three‑dimensional objects from a digital design by building them layer by layer. Instead of cutting material away (as in traditional manufacturing), 3D printers deposit or solidify materials such as plastics, resins, or powders under computer control to gradually form the desired shape. This technology allows for the production of complex designs, rapid prototyping, and customized items, and it is increasingly used across industries from healthcare to aerospace. You will be using this technology to create parts for your boardgame!
